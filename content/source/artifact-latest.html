My System Design Learning Hub

A personal textbook for system design, written in simple English. Each topic explains the idea, the problem it solves, how it works inside, the trade-offs, and how it shows up in interviews.

How to use this hub
  • Open a topic and read one section at a time. Sections are folded, so you can stop and come back.
  • Mark each topic as Not started, In progress, or Completed. Your progress is saved.
  • Use search or the status filters to find a topic quickly.
  • At the end of every topic, try the revision questions before opening the answers.
  • To add or change topics, ask in the chat. New topics are added without changing the old ones.
